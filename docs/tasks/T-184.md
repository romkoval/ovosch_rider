# T-184 — Road along the real route shape: projection, smoothing, curvature, height `[visual]`

Статус, приоритет и очередь — **только** в таблице `docs/backlog.md` (раздел 2). Здесь — описание, критерии приёмки, история и вердикты.

## Задача

- Executor: technical-artist. Size: L.
- Depends on: T-178 (spec), T-181 (route profile and coordinates). Н-78 (е) — 5 m / 10 m, 8 m, ±4 % are proposals.
- Why: owner decision 2026-10-10 (У-41) — the scene road follows the real route's turns and elevation. Today the road is a stylized loop (`loop_track.gd`, `profiled_track.gd`); stylized tracks must keep passing D3D-08.

## Что сделать

1. Local horizontal projection of the route (distance- and direction-preserving within tolerances), smoothing per the T-178 spec, minimum turn radius and banking limits.
2. Track implementation for catalog routes (alongside the stylized loop tracks): road axis position, azimuth and height h(s) at distance s, open-route return pass on the same road.
3. Road mesh along the real geometry (chunked so T-185 can stream it); rider pitch and camera on the tightest turn.
4. Frames: "climb" 500 / 1 500 / 1 950 m, "bend" 900 / 1 000 / 1 100 m via `./scripts/screenshot.sh` (before/after, `ride-visual-review`).

## Критерии приёмки

- REQ-D3D-11 p.10 (а) `[авто]` — road axis within 5 m of the projected polyline every 50 m (10 m at turns > 120°).
- REQ-D3D-11 p.10 (б) `[авто]` — axis length between points 1 km apart = Δs ± 0.5 %.
- REQ-D3D-11 p.10 (в) `[авто]` — azimuth ±3° where radius ≥ 30 m; turns ≥ 30° same direction and order; "bend" fixture north → right turn → east.
- REQ-D3D-11 p.10 (г) `[авто]` — radius ≥ 8 m, cross-slope within ±4 %, rider stays on the road 0–60 km/h.
- REQ-D3D-11 p.10 (д) `[авто]` — D3D-08 p.4, p.5 on "climb", "bend", 200 km fixtures; turns do not change the speed model.
- REQ-D3D-11 p.12 `[визуальная проверка]` (road part: climb up/flat, bend to the right, no kinks, gaps, overlaps, not sunk) — tester + game-designer.
- Regression: D3D-03, D3D-08 (four stylized tracks), D3D-05.
