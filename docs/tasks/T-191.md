## Что сделать

1. Shortlist of concrete routes (South China; islands) for the owner: place, start/finish, length, gain, max grade, why it is spectacular, which stylized environment set (D3D-08) fits or what new set it would need (flag for T-178).
2. For each route: OSM coverage check (road/track exists and is continuous in OSM; link to the OSM way/relation or a reproducible query), elevation source available (e.g. SRTM, Copernicus DEM) and its license.
3. License check: ODbL 1.0 obligations (attribution, share-alike of the derived route database) and the DEM license allow store distribution (D3D-11 p.3, NFR-07 p.4); routes failing it are dropped or flagged. Legal adequacy — owner's call.
4. Fit with D3D-11 limits (L ≥ 1 km, segment ≤ 50 m after resampling, |grade| clamp ±25 %, length vs the 200 km / 2 s threshold) and with the real-geometry road limits (hairpins, 8 m radius — Н-78 (е)).
5. Record the shortlist and the owner's confirmation in `docs/game/` (design.md or a routes section); hand the confirmed list to T-186.
