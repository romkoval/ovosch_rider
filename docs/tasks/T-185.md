# T-185 — Scenery along the route, overlapping sections, chunk streaming `[visual]`

Статус, приоритет и очередь — **только** в таблице `docs/backlog.md` (раздел 2). Здесь — описание, критерии приёмки, история и вердикты.

## Задача

- Executor: technical-artist. Size: L.
- Depends on: T-184, T-178 (scenery rules).
- Why: REQ-D3D-11 p.10 (е), (ж), p.12 — a real route is long and open; the world along it is built from the route's stylized assets and streamed within the D3D-05 / D3D-08 budgets.

## Что сделать

1. Scenery placement along the real road from the route's environment set (terrain, trees, roadside, landmarks per T-178); clearance from the road axis ≥ half-width + 1 m.
2. Overlapping sections (out-and-back, parallel passes within one road width) → a single road surface.
3. Chunk streaming of road and scenery: active chunk count bounded by a constant independent of route length (as D3D-03 p.2); no hitch when chunks stream in.
4. Budget checks every 50 m: MultiMesh instances, `MeshInstance3D` count, unique materials (`docs/perf_budget.md`).
5. Full p.12 frame set with scenery (before/after, `ride-visual-review`).

## Критерии приёмки

- REQ-D3D-11 p.10 (е) `[авто]` — scenery from the route world; nothing on the road; single surface on overlaps.
- REQ-D3D-11 p.10 (ж) `[авто]` — budgets every 50 m; bounded active chunks on the 200 km fixture.
- REQ-D3D-11 p.12 `[визуальная проверка]` — checklist ≥ 10 / 12, no blocking items on every frame (tester + game-designer).
- REQ-D3D-11 p.13 — `[ручная проверка]`, after T-186 real routes (section 4 of the backlog).
- Regression: D3D-03, D3D-05, D3D-07, D3D-08, D3D-10 (stylized tracks and conifers).
