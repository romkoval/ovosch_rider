# T-182 — GPS track of a route ride: samples, FIT position fields, Strava name `[integration]`

Статус, приоритет и очередь — **только** в таблице `docs/backlog.md` (раздел 2). Здесь — описание, критерии приёмки, история и вердикты.

## Задача

- Executor: developer. Size: M.
- Depends on: T-181. Н-78 (в) (VirtualRide), (е) (name/description rule) — proposals apply.
- Why: REQ-STR-06 (owner request 2026-10-10, У-39) — a ride on a catalog route shows the real route map in Strava. Coordinates are stored in the ride so its FIT never depends on the current catalog (D3D-11 p.9).

## Что сделать

1. Sample stream: every sample of a route ride holds lat/lon of the route point at its distance (incl. end-of-route handling); ride metadata holds route id and name.
2. FIT encoder: `record` fields 0/1 (`position_lat` / `position_long`, sint32 semicircles), `enhanced_altitude` = h(s); `session` fields 3/4 = first record; samples without distance → invalid per FIT spec, not 0/0; stylized tracks and no-track rides — no coordinates (as before).
3. Strava: default name for a free ride on a route — «Свободная езда — <маршрут>» / "Free ride — <route>"; plan on a route — plan name; description line «Маршрут: …» / "Route: …"; `sport=cycling`, `sub_sport=virtual_activity` unchanged.
4. Catalog independence: substituting a catalog without the route (or with changed data) leaves the saved ride, its history view and FIT byte-identical.

## Критерии приёмки

- REQ-STR-06 p.1–6 `[авто]` (p.6 — 10 min on "meridian" at 200 W → 600 records with coordinates, last record at the final distance ±1 m).
- REQ-D3D-11 p.9 `[авто]` — stable ids; saved rides, history (LOC-02, LOC-03) and FIT unchanged after a catalog change.
- REQ-STR-06 p.7 — `[ручная проверка]`, after T-186 real routes (section 4 of the backlog).
- Regression: LOC-01, LOC-05, FRD-07 p.5, STR-02, STR-03 (existing FIT and Strava tests stay green).
